# Security policy

Do not open public issues containing vulnerabilities, credentials, personal data, or production
details. Configure a private reporting address for each cloned product before launch and publish
its response expectations.

Treat exposed Django, database, Redis, Fly, object-storage, Sentry, email, payment, OAuth, and
field-encryption credentials as compromised: revoke/rotate them, preserve evidence, assess
personal-data impact, and follow the incident runbook.
