# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Accountants and their teams in Brazilian accounting offices (escritórios contábeis): owners, managers, fiscal, accounting and payroll operators, auditors. They work a portfolio of client companies by competência (the month being closed), against legal deadlines, usually on a desktop during office hours, often with the Domínio ERP open beside CICA. Many sessions are short and task-driven: classify this month's NFS-e, clear overdue work, check a client, download a package for the ERP.

## Product Purpose

CICA (by Mewstack) centralizes the office's operation by company, competência, owner and evidence: activities and deadlines, NFS-e collection and classification, tax guides and DCTFWeb, Caixa Postal DTE, bank reconciliation, document triage and a copilot. Success is the accountant opening a screen and knowing in seconds what to do next, finishing the month without hunting for information, and trusting that every number and status is real (sources, approvals and limits are explicit, never invented).

## Brand Personality

Calm, exact, unhurried confidence. The voice states facts and next actions in short Portuguese; numbers and dates carry the state. Three words: precise, quiet, trustworthy. The interface never explains itself in prose; labels are short nouns, buttons are verbs.

## Anti-references

- Screens that teach in paragraphs ("Veja o movimento, a contraparte…", "Escolha uma prioridade e encontre…").
- Dashboard templates with a hero-metric strip and cards for everything.
- Several primary buttons competing on one screen; downloads louder than the work.
- Hidden filters behind "Refinar" disclosures; menus clipped inside scroll containers.
- Generic SaaS cream/sand palettes and uppercase eyebrows above every section.
- Positive references: Linear and Stripe Dashboard for list toolbars and status tabs; Trello for boards; Qive for fiscal-document lists.

## Design Principles

1. The work comes first: the list of items to act on appears near the top; summaries, reports and downloads are secondary.
2. One primary action per screen; everything else is quiet.
3. State is shown, not explained: counts, dates, badges and the record identifier carry meaning.
4. Truth over comfort: show source, approval and limits; never imply an import, payment or legal deadline that was not confirmed.
5. Filters are visible, live and reflected in the URL; an empty result always offers the way out.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA: 4.5:1 text, 3:1 large text and controls; visible focus ring on every focusable element; targets ≥24px (44px on touch); keyboard paths (Tab, Esc, arrows in radio groups and tabs); color never the only carrier of meaning; dark and light themes from semantic tokens; respects reduced motion; works at 375px without horizontal page scroll.
